Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 14, 2025

We value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our Pinterest app for Pin creation, reporting, and ad campaign management.

1. Information We Collect​

We collect data through Pinterest's official API with your consent, including:

  • Your Pinterest account information (e.g., username, profile ID)
  • Pins and board metadata (for scheduling and content publishing)
  • Ad campaign details and performance data
  • Reporting metrics (analytics, engagement data)

We do not collect passwords or other sensitive login credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data​

We use your data to:

  • Automate and schedule Pin creation
  • Generate reports and performance insights
  • Manage and optimize ad campaigns

All operations are performed securely through Pinterest's developer platform.

3. Who We Share Data With​

We do not share or sell your data to third parties. Data is only used for your account’s functionality and is stored securely.

4. Data Storage & Security​

  • We store data temporarily for processing purposes only.
  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • You may request deletion of your data at any time.

5. Your Rights​

You may:

  • Disconnect your Pinterest account at any time
  • Request data access or deletion by contacting us
  • Withdraw consent for any data processing

6. Audience​

Our app is intended for:

  • Pinners (general Pinterest users)
  • Creators (users who publish content)
  • Advertisers (users managing Pinterest Ads)

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Contact Us​

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:

📧 [threecats2k@gmail.com]
🌐 [threecats2k.github.io]

By using our app, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.
