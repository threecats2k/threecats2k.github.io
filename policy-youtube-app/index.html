Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 14, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use our app to upload videos to YouTube through Google Cloud APIs.

1. Information We Collect​

When you use our app and grant access via your Google Account, we may collect the following:

  • Your Google account identifier (basic profile info)
  • OAuth tokens for accessing the YouTube Data API
  • Metadata about videos you upload (title, description, category, privacy settings)
  • Uploaded video content

We do not collect or store your Google password or other sensitive credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data​

Your data is used only to provide the functionality of the app, which includes:

  • Uploading videos to your YouTube channel
  • Managing video metadata (title, description, privacy settings)
  • Providing feedback on upload status

We do not use your data for advertising or analytics.

3. How We Share Your Data​

We do not share, sell, or transfer your personal information or video content to any third party. Data is used only for the intended purpose of interacting with YouTube through your account.

4. Google API Services Disclosure​

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data Retention and Deletion​

  • Access tokens and user data are stored temporarily during your session and are deleted afterward.
  • You may revoke the app’s access to your Google Account at any time via Google Account Permissions.
  • We do not store your uploaded videos or metadata beyond what is necessary to complete the upload process.

6. Security​

We use modern security best practices including:

  • OAuth 2.0 for authentication
  • HTTPS encryption for data in transit
  • No long-term storage of personal data

7. Your Rights​

You may:

  • Revoke access at any time
  • Request deletion of any temporarily stored data
  • Contact us about data practices at the email below

8. Children's Privacy​

This service is not intended for children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Contact​

If you have any questions about this policy or our data practices, please contact:

📧 doanthai0111@gmail.com

By using our app, you consent to this Privacy Policy and the way we process your data as described above.
